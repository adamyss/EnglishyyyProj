About the Simulation

The simulation tests evaluate personal abilities, choices, and fears, to help lead them to their ideal faction within the society. The trials often blur the boundaries of real life, creating intense environments that sometimes traumatize the participants. The simulation and factions are NECESSARY to maintain a properly working society without the risk of another war.

Factions

Abnegation

Location: Add the Abnegation location here.

Faction color: Grey.

“I choose to turn away from my reflection, to rely not on myself but on my brothers and sisters, to project always outward until I disappear.”

Description: Abnegation values the needs of others above the needs of oneself. Its core belief is “Them before I.” Members take on the city's public services. The society is ruled by a council of fifty people, composed entirely of Abnegation representatives, whose commitment to selflessness is seen as making them the least corruptible.

Notable book members

  • Marcus Eaton
  • Andrew and Natalie Prior

What they blame the war on:
The selfishness and manipulation of the Erudite faction.

Erudite

Location: Add the Erudite location here.

Faction color: Blue.

“Ignorance is defined not as stupidity but as lack of knowledge. Lack of knowledge inevitably leads to a lack of understanding. Lack of understanding leads to a disconnect among people with differences. Disconnection among people with differences leads to conflict. Knowledge is the only logical solution to the problem of conflict. Therefore, we propose that to eliminate conflict, we must eliminate the disconnect among those with differences by correcting the lack of understanding that arises from ignorance with knowledge.”

Description: Erudite values intelligence, knowledge, and curiosity. Its priority on knowledge is also tied to ethics and power: in the book, some members become increasingly greedy for power as a result of their pursuit of knowledge.

Notable book members

  • Jeanine Matthews (Divergent)
  • Caleb Prior
  • Cara

What they blame the war on: Ignorance.

Dauntless

Location: Add the Dauntless location here.

Faction color: Black.

“We believe in ordinary acts of bravery, in the courage that drives one person to stand up for another... We believe in shouting for those who can only whisper, in defending those who cannot protect themselves.”

Description: Dauntless are the soldiers of the factions. They value bravery and strive to be fearless, courageous, and durable. They guard the Fence to make sure no one gets in or out. Initiates prove their bravery through challenges such as jumping from moving trains to reach the Dauntless base.

Amity

Location: Add the Amity location here.

Faction color: Red.

“Give freely, trusting that you will be given what you need... Do not be angry. The opinions of others cannot damage you... The wrong is past. You must let it rest where it lies... You must no longer think cruel thoughts. Cruel thoughts lead to cruel words and hurt you as much as they wound their target.”

Description: Amity values peace and harmony. Its members are happy and willing to serve. The faction requires them to wear some of its colors, and unlike Abnegation, they usually serve people without meeting them face-to-face.

Notable book members

  • Johanna Reyes
  • Robert Black

What they blame the war on: Aggression.

Candor

Location: Add the Candor location here.

Faction colors: Black and white.

“Dishonesty is rampant. Dishonesty is temporary. Dishonesty makes evil possible.”

Description: Candor values honesty above all else. Its members wear black and white to symbolize their belief that things are true or false, with no grey areas. Many leaders come from Candor because of the faction's reputation for trustworthiness.

Notable book members

  • Jack Kang

What they blame the war on: Dishonesty and deception.

Factionless

Description: Factionless people do not belong to any faction. The book describes their lives as similar to homelessness and full of suffering. Abnegation provides them with food and medical attention, while the other factions usually ignore them.

“The factionless are the ghosts of our society, haunting the streets we pretend do not exist while we live our lives in blissful ignorance.”

Divergent

Description: Divergent people do not fit into one faction and instead share traits, choices, or ideologies from multiple factions. They are considered dangerous because they disrupt society's natural order, so their status is hidden from the general public and they are taken away if discovered.

Notable book members

  • Tobias (Four) Eaton
